## Official usage and scope

- Wolt Blue anchors consumer communications and remains present, even as a small detail. Keep its appearance consistent.
- Accent colors are limited highlights for selected elements, not an instruction to use every accent together. Do not use accent colors for text.
- In consumer graphic layouts, Wolt Blue occupies about 80% of the available area **excluding photography and 3D**. Do not impose an 80% blue-area quota on a photographed/generated interior, food or skin.
- Wolt Partner communications use predominantly Blueberry Blue, approximately 70%, with Wolt Blue taking the remaining 30%; Milk White and Wolt Blue serve text/graphic elements over the dark background. This partner-layout rule is not the default for these consumer food ads.
- Graphic frames are white; the source does not authorize colored frames. This concerns graphic treatment, not the color of physical furniture.

## Interior application — user preference and project direction

The user explicitly chooses light, restrained Wolt-color accents for interiors, with NO percentage quota and NO requirement for a predominantly blue room. Use small touches in a chair, textile, lamp, vase or other set dressing; colored objects do not need a logo. A large blue wall, cabinet run or tabletop is not the default. Name the chosen official color and HEX in the prompt. Do not call generic cobalt, teal, coral or mint an official Wolt color unless it matches the named palette above.

Choose a coherent overall scene palette according to the meal, then add a light branded detail if useful. A small Wolt Blue prop beside warm golden pizza or orange-red sauce can establish the brand; a restrained Raspberry Pink or Lime Green detail is another option. Do not assemble every brand color or make branded color coverage a composition target. Milk White tableware can keep the food readable; Blueberry Blue works as a limited darker detail without turning the entire high-key scene dark. Light accents can support the set, but do not wash the whole scene into pale pastels. These are optional examples, not a mandatory identical set for every story.

Preserve natural food/skin color, true texture and bright high-key volume. Avoid colored light spilling over the food. Brand color in decor is distinct from brand logo placement: the Wolt wordmark remains only the authentic print on the hero kraft bag, never on the wall, as an overlay or in a logo-only panel. The final pack shot remains Wolt Blue `#00C2E8`, with the loaded bag surrounded by the scenario's delivered items.


For reusable project-authored combinations and style-reference boundaries, use [color-schemes](color-schemes.md). Style examples never override the official Wolt palette. Interior accents have no percentage quota; natural food/skin/kraft colors remain unchanged.
