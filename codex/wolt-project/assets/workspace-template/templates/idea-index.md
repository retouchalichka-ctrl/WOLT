## Definitions

- `inbox`: captured but not selected for development.
- `active`: being developed, reviewed, generated or delivered.
- `archive`: closed for any reason, including successful delivery, rejection, merge or pause.
- Archive is not synonymous with failure. Read the outcome review and evidence.
