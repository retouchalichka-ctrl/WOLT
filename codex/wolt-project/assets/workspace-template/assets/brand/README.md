## Courier

- `courier/courier-01.webp`
- `courier/courier-02.jpg`

Use only for clothing silhouette, Wolt Blue and delivery-gear geometry. Do not copy face identity, location or photographic style. Suppress all visible courier logos/text in generated footage so only the kraft bag remains branded.

When a courier appears in storyboard/video, frame every shot below the chin. No face, eyes, mouth, full chin or recognizable identity may enter the frame. Prefer future approved equipment-only or pre-cropped references to reduce identity leakage.
