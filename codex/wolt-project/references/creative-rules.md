# Creative Rules

These bundled rules are sufficient in standalone mode. In a managed workspace, read its current `docs/brand/WOLT_RULES.md`, `docs/creative/VISUAL_LANGUAGE_LIBRARY.md`, or `docs/production/REFERENCE_POLICY.md` only when concept-specific detail or a newer client override is needed. `wolt-project` is the sole domain skill; never load or read `wolt-dop` or `seedance-2-5-dop`.

## Timing

- Deliverable: `15s`, `16:9`, one complete multi-shot Seedance prompt/generation by default.
- `0–3s`: the hook must already work visually, not merely start setting up. Early bag/logo is preferred by default; when the user/client requests it, recognizable Wolt bag/logo exposure within 0–3s is mandatory and built into the action. A background bag or brief moving glimpse is valid if the brand registers at normal playback; do not require a close-up or separate hold. A blue accent or an unrecognizable flash alone does not fulfill that request.
- `3–6s`: dynamic escalation/turn; the client prefers the main action and decisive reveal to happen within the first 6s. Keep the geography readable; dynamic does not require arbitrary cuts. Do not defer the core story to the second half.
- `6–15s`: consequence, resolution, food/product beauty, Wolt pack shot, and clean post-copy space.

## Final pack shot

The final frame is a pack shot of the loaded Wolt kraft bag against Wolt Blue #00C2E8, surrounded by an attractive arrangement of delivered food, groceries or goods selected for this scenario. Name the actual items in the prompt and preserve their identity from the story; never finish on a lone bag or add unrelated products. Keep the logo readable, realistic product contact/shadows, high-key texture and clean space for post copy. Other packaging remains generic and textless; food/Pharmacy constraints still apply. Carry this ending into any storyboard panel depicting the final state and the timed Seedance final beat, unless the user/client explicitly requests another ending.

## Bright high-key advertising image

The client likes bright, juicy advertising visuals in high-key lighting: a luminous scene, generous soft fill/open shadows, clean saturated colors and controlled specular highlights. Retain dimensional separation, appetizing food texture, kraft fibers and legible bag print. Do not equate this with clipping whites, washed-out pastels, flat exposure, bloom everywhere or a mandatory white studio. Motivate bright light within the chosen location; Wolt Blue stays clean and food color believable.

Art-direct interiors with a deliberate saturated palette that contrasts with the hero food: choose one dominant background hue and one restrained accent, and name them in the image/video prompts. For branded set details, select named HEX colors from [brand-colors](brand-colors.md): a small Wolt Blue, Raspberry Pink or Lime Green detail can complement the food contrast. Brand colors are light accents, not a requirement to make the room predominantly blue; no percentage rule applies to the interior. Generic cobalt/teal/mint are creative color descriptions, not additional official Wolt colors. These are scenario-dependent choices, not mandatory colors for every film. Avoid default beige-on-beige rooms and washed-out lifestyle lighting; retain natural food/skin color and appetizing texture without colored spill contaminating the meal. The final pack shot still uses Wolt Blue #00C2E8.

The Wolt wordmark appears only as authentic print physically on the hero bag. No standalone logo, logo-only panel, title card, watermark, floating wordmark or background/overlay logo in any storyboard panel or video frame, including the final pack shot.

## Dynamic advertising

Every shot changes information, action, emotion, scale, or product value. Motivate camera movement by reveal, pursuit, scale, emotion, or rhythm. Use complementary cuts and synchronized Foley rather than random transition packs.

## Audio and information graphics

Default audio is an instrumental music bed plus precise synchronized SFX/Foley for contacts, food texture, bag paper, movement, and transitions. Include no dialogue, VO, spoken words, vocal phrases, vocals, or lyrics unless explicitly requested.

Default visuals contain no infographic or explainer layer: no charts, diagrams, arrows, UI panels, callouts, labels, icons, or informational overlays unless explicitly requested. Artistic VFX is allowed when it belongs to the physical scene and cause-and-effect story rather than explaining information.

## Food-porn preference

For Restaurants and suitable Groceries, favor intensely appetizing advertising craft: macro proof of steam, crispness, moist cut surfaces, controlled gloss/viscosity, crumbs, freshness, utensil contact, and synchronized crunch/sizzle/pour Foley. Return from extreme detail to a clear hero dish and Wolt role by 3–6 seconds. Describe the concrete sensory traits in final prompts instead of relying on the phrase `food porn`. Choose the halal-safe dish before styling it; never hide a prohibited ingredient with framing.

## Shot-size and angle harmony

Use the working ladder `ECU/detail → CU → MCU → MS → MLS → WS → EWS`.

For an ordinary cut, prefer:

- a change of at least two ladder positions, skipping the adjacent size;
- a meaningful angle, height, or axis change, often around 30°+ but judged visually;
- preserved action phase, screen direction, eye trace, and geography.

This is artistic grammar, not arithmetic. A motivated exception is allowed when it clearly looks stronger and does not cause an accidental jump cut, visual rejection, or loss of comprehension. Record the reason in the scenario/shot plan and review the actual storyboard/video by eye.

## Named inspirations

Use creator/studio names only during internal research. Select at most one primary and one supporting visual language, translate them into observable craft traits, and omit all names and signature-shot imitation from final prompts.

## Wolt invariants

The Wolt bag always contains actual products appropriate to the scene; never depict it empty, even after an item is removed. When its interior is visible, show remaining products or their generic textless containers, not a hollow cavity or bare bottom. A closed bag still has believable loaded volume, weight, base contact and handle tension; it need not be opened or overfilled. Preserve the remaining contents across cuts. A bag reference controls its design, not an empty interior.

The kraft bag is the only branded generated object. Everything else is generic, textless, and unbranded. Generate no copy. Apply client halal rules to the full frame, not just the hero dish.

- Wolt Blue `#00C2E8`; Blueberry Blue `#021738`; Milk White `#F8F8F8`.
- No pork, alcohol or alcohol context, molluscs/shellfish, ambiguous meat, or meat and dairy in one dish/meal setup.
- A meat burger has no cheese or dairy sauce. Pizza is margherita, four-cheese or explicitly vegetarian, with no meat or seafood.
- Apply these checks to foreground food, background tables, shelves, refrigerators and visible packaging.

Courier/equipment references may control clothing silhouette, Wolt Blue, and delivery-gear geometry. Never show a courier face: keep the top edge of every courier storyboard panel and video shot below the chin, excluding eyes, mouth, full chin, and recognizable facial identity. Make the crop intentional around torso, hands, motion, and gear. Suppress all visible courier logos/text unless a client override explicitly changes the bag-only rule.

## Pharmacy in standalone mode

Without explicit market/approval information, use neutral adult wellness or generic skincare, with no medical promise. Do not show close-up pills/tablets, needles/syringes/IV, prescription names, diagnosis imagery, before/after or dramatic body transformation, surgery, pain/distress as a sales device, guaranteed cure claims or minors using health products. OTC products, devices, healthcare professionals and licensed locations require the applicable explicit approvals; their presence in a reference is not permission. Read the full client brand rules only when a specific permitted exception needs clarification.

## Creative engine: surprise with a reason

Start with one recognizable desire or tiny frustration, one unexpected visual rule and one satisfying delivery consequence. Wolt enables access, convenience, anticipation or a shared moment. The bag need not be a magic switch in every idea: physical delivery and human behavior can carry the story.

Before presenting, answer silently: what changes in the first visible instant; what does the viewer expect; how does the turn change that expectation; why does Wolt matter; what earns the remaining seconds? If the answer needs explanatory copy, simplify the visible cause and effect. Sound adds pleasure, but a sound-led concept still has a readable muted version.

For a list, vary both human situation and story engine: observation/comic timing, anticipation, tactile satisfaction, scale/perception, social reaction, a physical chain reaction. These are options, not quotas. Reject renamed repetitions of “bag opens → magical spread → pack shot”. A different VFX treatment alone is not a different story.

Choose one reason to replay/share: a revealed hidden cause, relatable behavior, a satisfying completion or an earned visual loop. Do not stack all devices, add empty cliffhangers or promise virality. A loop must match position, motion, exposure and sound across the seam without sacrificing Wolt recognition.

## Details that survive generation

Give the signature action concrete start/contact/end states. Track only scene-critical counts, hand entry, screen direction, bag orientation and product state. Choose 2–3 sensory details true to this food/product and time Foley to the contacts. Do not sprinkle steam, gloss or crumbs on everything.

One dominant unusual mechanism is a useful default. Multiple transformations, moving hands, a rotating branded bag and a moving camera at the same moment increase continuity risk; separate their beats or use one motivated occlusion. Keep the surprise and simplify its execution before inflating the negative prompt. Record one simpler fallback only for a material risk, not for every concept.

A first-frame hook is an action or a visible anomaly, not an establishing shot followed by a promised event. Resolve the main premise by 6s under the default brief, then use consequence, reaction or sensory proof to keep 6–15s active. Add an exact final frame with a clear Wolt memory. A seamless loop is optional, not a universal finish.

One focused creative check is enough: muted clarity, causal Wolt role, a distinct turn, readable timing, feasible contacts and applicable compliance. Fix concrete failures once; no numeric self-scoring ritual or exhaustive hidden variant tournament.

## Photoreal finish

Aim for camera-believable premium advertising stills and video: true food/skin/kraft texture, coherent shadows/reflections, believable weight, contact, inertia and natural motion. High-key must retain volume and surface detail. Avoid plastic CGI, waxy food, oversharpened edges, smeared motion or texture/identity flicker. A surreal story rule is allowed; its materials and execution should look convincingly photographed. Describe visible material/light/contact behavior instead of stacking “8K, masterpiece, ultra” adjectives.

## Appetite and food cinematography

For food-led ads, making the viewer want this specific food is a creative objective. Build the hook/payoff around one appetizing event, not an interchangeable beauty montage. Pair a legible hero-wide composition showing the dish and Wolt context with selective juicy macro when the scenario benefits; either can carry the opening action. Preserve food identity, portion and ingredient continuity between scales. Do not impose food imagery on Pets or Pharmacy.

Translate references to Daniel Schiffer or other commercial food filmmakers into observable techniques: tactile close-focus passes, controlled push-ins, short parallax arcs, action-matched cuts and a brief speed change around a satisfying contact. Names are creative shorthand, not a complete prompt or a reason to reproduce an existing ad. Specify the camera's starting view, path, landing composition and focus target. One dominant move per beat; use a clear wide reveal to restore geography after macro.

Choose 2–3 truthful appetite cues: a crisp crust breaking, moist crumb separating, fresh cut fruit, a viscous dairy-free sauce settling, or a restrained cheese pull on an allowed vegetarian dish. Steam only from hot food, condensation only where physically plausible. Avoid greasy excess, rubbery stretches, plastic gloss, raw centers and impossible splashes. Soft side/back light may reveal texture while fill keeps the high-key scene open. Keep the food readable through the move; brief tactile slow motion earns its time, but prolonged slow motion must not delay the turn beyond 6s. Foley reinforces the exact break/pour/contact; the image remains appetizing muted.


For reusable project-authored combinations and style-reference boundaries, use [color-schemes](color-schemes.md). Style examples never override the official Wolt palette. Interior accents have no percentage quota; natural food/skin/kraft colors remain unchanged.


## Explicit food lock — 2026-09-09

Food lock for every standalone storyboard and Seedance prompt: name each permitted dish and its ingredients, then include a concise exclusion applying to every frame, background, bag contents and final pack shot: “No pork or pork derivatives, pepperoni, salami, bacon, ham, molluscs/shellfish, alcohol, or meat combined with dairy.” Do not rely on “halal food”, a generic “pizza/burger/assorted food”, or “same as reference”. These are this project's client restrictions, not a universal definition of halal.

Never use pepperoni (including a supposed halal/beef substitute) or cheeseburgers. Pizza: explicitly meat-free margherita/four-cheese/vegetable pizza, no seafood. Meat burger: halal beef/chicken patty, dairy-free bun and sauce, lettuce/tomato/pickles; no cheese, butter, cream or other dairy. Keep the existing prohibition on meat+dairy in a shared meal setup. Do not introduce cheese slices, cheese pulls, creamy dairy sauces or dairy garnish into a meat dish. A cheese pull is only for an explicitly permitted meat-free dish. Prohibited food visible in a reference is not permission: exclude it from reference scope and specify a safe replacement. Check all visible dishes and sauces before handoff and any received render; preserve originals and revise failed outputs before further use.
